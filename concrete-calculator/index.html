Privacy Policy

Concrete Calculator · com.ashraf.concrete_calculator
Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Concrete Calculator mobile application (the “App”), developed by Ashraf (the “Developer”), handles your information. The App is an offline construction estimating tool. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal or sensitive user data. The App has no user accounts, no login, and does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or any third-party software development kits.

Data stored on your device

The App stores the following only on your device, never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your preferences (language, theme, measurement units, cement bag weight, default waste allowance, numeral style).
  • Your saved calculation history (dimensions, volumes, material estimates, and optional price you enter).

This information remains on your device. You can delete it at any time by using the in-app “Clear all” / delete controls, by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

Permissions

The App does not request internet access and does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or any other personal information.

Clipboard

If you tap the “Copy” button, a text summary of your current calculation is placed on your device’s clipboard for your convenience. This happens only when you choose it, and the data is not sent anywhere.

Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Third-party services

The App does not use any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics providers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
