Privacy Policy

Dem | دەم · com.ashraf.dem
Last updated: 1 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Dem | دەم mobile application (the “App”), developed by Shark Team (shark-team.com, the “Developer”), handles your information. The App shows Islamic prayer times, plays the adhan, points to the Qibla, and includes the Qur’an, duas and adhkar. The Developer does not collect, receive, store or share any of your personal data. The App has no user accounts, no login, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the Developer runs no servers that receive data from it.

Location

With your permission, the App uses your device’s location to calculate prayer times for where you are and the direction of the Qibla. This calculation happens entirely on your device, and your location is saved only on your device. It is never sent to the Developer.

  • To display the name of your city, the App asks your device’s built-in geocoding service to look up the coordinates. On Android this service is provided by Google, which receives your approximate coordinates for that lookup under Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Location permission is optional. You can choose your city manually instead, and you can turn the permission off at any time in your device settings.

Qibla map (the only part that uses the internet)

Everything in the App works offline, including prayer times, the Qur’an, the compass and the adhan. The only exception is the optional Qibla map screen. When you open it, the App downloads map images from OpenStreetMap tile servers and the open-source Leaflet map library from unpkg.com. As with any web request, these servers receive your IP address and which map area is being shown, which is the area around your location. The App sends them nothing else. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy. If you never open the Qibla map, the App makes no internet connections.

Notifications and the adhan

Adhan notifications are scheduled locally on your device from the calculated prayer times. The App does not use any push-notification service and does not create a notification token. A foreground service is used only while the adhan audio is playing.

Data stored on your device

The App stores the following only on your device, never transmitted to the Developer:

  • Your settings: language, theme, calculation method, Asr method, manual time adjustments, adhan sound and notification preferences.
  • Your location or the city you chose, used for prayer times and the Qibla.
  • Your tasbih counter, Qur’an bookmarks and last reading position.
  • The prayers you mark as prayed in the prayer tracker.
  • Prayer times and dates prepared for the home-screen and lock-screen widgets.

You can delete this information at any time by clearing the App’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. If you have turned on Android’s automatic backup, Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings, not by the Developer.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate) — to calculate prayer times and the Qibla for where you are.
  • Notifications — to show the adhan at each prayer time.
  • Exact alarms — so the adhan sounds exactly at the prayer time.
  • Foreground service (media playback) — to keep playing the adhan audio.
  • Vibration — for the tasbih counter and adhan alerts.
  • Internet — only for the optional Qibla map described above.

The App does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or any other personal information.

Sharing and selling of data

The Developer does not sell, rent, trade or share any user data, because the Developer does not receive any. The App contains no advertising networks, analytics providers or other third-party software that collects data.

Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Security

Data the App saves stays inside its private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.
